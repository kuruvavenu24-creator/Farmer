Agriculture and farmers support food, work, and village life

Agriculture is one of the most important parts of daily life. It provides food, creates rural jobs, supports local markets, and keeps communities strong. Farmers work with land, water, weather, seeds, and time to grow crops that feed families across the country.

This portal is built to support farmers with simple digital tools. Instead of showing every tool directly on the home page, each feature now has its own page so the site is easier to understand and use.

About Farmers

Hard work in every season

Farmers manage sowing, irrigation, pest control, crop care, and harvest while adjusting to changing climate and market conditions.

Smart decisions matter

Good information about temperature, rainfall, soil, and crop suitability can reduce risk and improve productivity in the field.

Technology can help

Weather reports, crop tools, yield estimates, and field maps help farmers plan their work more clearly and save time.
