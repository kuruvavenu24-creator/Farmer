Crop Seasons and Farming Calendar

Kharif Crops

Sown during monsoon (June-July) and harvested in September-October.

Examples: Rice, Cotton, Maize, Sugarcane

Rabi Crops

Sown in winter (October-November) and harvested in March-April.

Examples: Wheat, Mustard, Barley

Zaid Crops

Grown between Kharif and Rabi seasons.

Examples: Watermelon, Cucumber, Vegetables
